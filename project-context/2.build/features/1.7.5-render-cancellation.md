# 1.7.5 — Render Cancellation (S3)

Status: Implemented and automatically verified; real Extension Host smoke pending.
Authorized: 2026-10-17, “Pode tratar o achado 3”.

## Scope and Contract

The render coordinator discards a cancelled generation's result or ordinary error
when its producer ignores the abort signal. Cancellation advances the public request
identity, clears queued work and retains cooperative abort. Subsequent requests still
render, including requests scheduled synchronously by an abort listener. Burst
coalescing and normal current-generation error delivery remain supported.

The fix belongs in `src/revisionGraph/renderCoordinator.ts`; existing controller,
loader and S2 origin checks remain additional boundaries. The original finding
proved a coordinator-contract defect; a stale visual update after closing a panel
was not established. The broader S4 E2E expansion remains separate work.

## Implementation and Review

- Increment the generation before clearing pending work and firing abort listeners.
  A listener can synchronously schedule a fresh request; invalidating after abort
  would incorrectly retire that new request.
- Before `onResult` or `onError`, check both generation ownership and the producer's
  abort signal. A cancelled result/error becomes `discarded` independently of how
  the producer handles cancellation.
- Keep the serial drain and outcome cleanup. Cancelling does not force a producer's
  promise to settle: callers and a queued replacement still wait for the active
  producer to settle. This change prevents stale delivery rather than adding a
  separate concurrent-render or timeout model.

Source review checked the synchronous ordering around abort listeners, absence of
an asynchronous gap between delivery guards and callbacks, preservation of current
errors/coalescing, and outcome-map cleanup. The production change is two predicates
and generation invalidation; no new allocation, module, dependency, protocol,
manifest, version change or quality exception is introduced. Controlled promises
provide deterministic evidence for this bounded contract; no Jev judgment was needed.
